53W

Set on top of the MOMA in Jean Nouvel's iconic highrise in NY, this Penthouse has infinity ebony floors which disappear into the night sky. The cascading hallways are dressed in Atelier Fevrier silk rugs and art niches. The Raku-Yaki bar stands tall surrounded by pieces by Vincenzo De Cotiis. The design of this space was done respecting the environment of art-meets-architecture.
