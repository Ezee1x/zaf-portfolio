New York-based and internationally experienced, Zafrin Hossain is an architectural designer focused on high-end retail, commercial, residential, and hospitality environments, guiding projects from early strategy to final delivery.

Her practice includes sustainable design, construction drawings and detailing, 3D and physical modeling, BIM workflows, and rendered imagery that supports project development with clarity and precision.

Originally from Bangladesh, she brings an artistic perspective shaped by her roots and refined by the pace of New York City, creating spaces that feel elegant, functional, and context-driven.
